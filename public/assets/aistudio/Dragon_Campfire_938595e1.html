🔥 Dragon Campfire Defense

Night after night, monsters pour out of four dark caves to snuff out your campfire. Hatch dragons, raid the caves and survive to wave 15 to face the Nightmare Lord.
Goal: keep the campfire lit, destroy the caves, beat the boss.
Move: WASD / arrows, or drag on the screen (touch).
Eggs: grab wild eggs in the forest and carry them to the campfire to hatch dragons. Far eggs are stronger but guarded.
Raid (R): your dragons follow you, fight the monsters hunting you and smash caves. Off at night.
Auto-walk (E / 🧭): walks you to the nearest cave with your dragons.
📍: turns the off-screen cave arrows on/off.
Map (G): shows all 4 caves. Mute (M) · Pause (P / Esc)
Every 5th wave is a night: be home by the fire. Survive it and pick an upgrade at dawn.
